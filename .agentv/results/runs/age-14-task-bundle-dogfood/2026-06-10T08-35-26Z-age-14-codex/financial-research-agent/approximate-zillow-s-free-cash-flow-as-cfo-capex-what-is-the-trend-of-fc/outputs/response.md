Using FCF = CFO - purchases of property & equipment:

| Year | Revenue | CFO | Capex | FCF | FCF margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023 | $1,945M | $354M | $135M | $219M | 11.3% |
| 2024 | $2,236M | $428M | $143M | $285M | 12.7% |
| 2025 | $2,583M | $368M | $133M | $235M | 9.1% |

Trend: Zillow’s FCF margin improved from about 11% in 2023 to about 13% in 2024, then fell to about 9% in 2025. The 2025 decline was mainly from lower CFO despite revenue growth.

Evidence: Zillow’s 2024 10-K reports 2023/2024 revenue, CFO, and property/equipment purchases; its 2025 release reports 2025 revenue, CFO, and capex figures. Sources: SEC 2024 10-K lines for revenue and cash flow, and 2025 PRNewswire release lines for 2025 financials/cash-flow reconciliation.
