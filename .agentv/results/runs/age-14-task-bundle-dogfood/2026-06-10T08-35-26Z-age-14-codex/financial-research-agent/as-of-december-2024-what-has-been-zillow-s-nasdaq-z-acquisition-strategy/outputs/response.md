As of December 2024, Zillow’s acquisition strategy was targeted, software-led, and asset-light, not a return to iBuying.

Over the prior two years it bought:

- **VRX Media** in Dec. 2022: listing media services, drone photography, virtual staging, 3D tours, and photographer network, folded into **ShowingTime+** to improve premium listing products. Zillow said this would support Listing Media Services and Listing Showcase. [Zillow IR](https://investors.zillowgroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2022/Zillow-Group-acquires-VRX-Media-to-create-national-photographer-network-elevate-listing-media-through-ShowingTime-brand/default.aspx)
- **Aryeo** in July 2023: real estate photographer software, for about **$35 million**. [Zillow 2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764025000016/z-20241231.htm)
- **Spruce** in Sept. 2023: tech-enabled title and escrow assets, for about **$19 million**. [Zillow 2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764025000016/z-20241231.htm)
- **Follow Up Boss** in Dec. 2023: real estate CRM, for **$399 million cash net of cash acquired** plus up to **$100 million** contingent consideration. Zillow described it as part of a “more integrated software experience.” [Zillow 2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764025000016/z-20241231.htm)

Strategically, these deals build tools around agents, listings, CRM, transaction workflow, and closing rather than buying homes. That aligns with Zillow’s revenue mix: **Residential/For Sale remains the core**, but the mix is becoming more platform-like, with software, rentals, and mortgages growing around the home transaction.

Numeric alignment in 2024:

- **Total revenue:** **$2.236B**, up **15%**
- **Residential:** **$1.594B**, up **10%**; about **71%** of revenue
- **Mortgages:** **$145M**, up **51%**
- **Rentals:** **$453M**, up **27%**
- **For Sale revenue** = Residential + Mortgages: **$1.739B**, up **12%** [Zillow Q4 2024 results](https://zillowgroup.mediaroom.com/2025-02-11-Zillow-Group-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2024-Financial-Results)

So the acquisition pattern supports Zillow’s shift from a lead-generation marketplace toward an integrated housing transaction platform: agent SaaS and listing media strengthen Residential, Spruce supports closing workflows, and the broader mix increasingly includes faster-growing Rentals and Mortgages.
