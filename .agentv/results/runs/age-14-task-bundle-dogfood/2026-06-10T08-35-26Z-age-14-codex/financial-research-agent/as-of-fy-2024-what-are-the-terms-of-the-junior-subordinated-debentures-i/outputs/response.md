As of FY 2024, Allstate had **$500 million** outstanding of **Series A 6.500% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Junior Subordinated Debentures**.

Key terms:

- **Principal outstanding:** $500 million
- **Coupon:** 6.500% fixed, payable semi-annually until **May 15, 2037**
- **Floating rate after May 15, 2037:** quarterly at **3-month CME Term SOFR + 0.26161% spread adjustment + 2.120%**
- **Scheduled maturity:** **May 15, 2057**
- **Final maturity:** **May 15, 2067**
- **Optional redemption:** redeemable in whole or part on/after **May 15, 2037** at principal plus accrued/unpaid interest
- **Pre-2037 redemption:** allowed in certain circumstances at principal plus accrued/unpaid interest, or if greater, a make-whole price
- **Interest deferral:** Allstate may defer interest for consecutive periods up to **10 years**; deferred interest compounds
- **Dividend stopper:** if interest is deferred, Allstate generally cannot pay dividends/distributions on common or preferred stock or repurchase/redeem those shares, subject to limited exceptions
- **Replacement capital covenant:** Allstate generally cannot repay/redeem/purchase the debentures before **May 15, 2067** unless it raises qualifying replacement capital, subject to exceptions; current covered debt is its **$800 million Floating Rate Subordinated Debentures due 2053**

Source: Allstate FY 2024 Form 10-K, Note 14, “Capital Structure,” SEC filing: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/899051/000089905125000015/all-20241231.htm
