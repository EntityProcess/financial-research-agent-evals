Yes. Workday reports a **gross revenue retention rate** in its quarterly and annual reporting. I did not find Workday reporting a **net revenue retention** metric in the actual 10-Q/10-K reporting reviewed, though it described both gross and net retention definitions in SEC comment-response correspondence.

**Reported metric:** Gross revenue retention rate  
**Latest reported value found:** approximately **97% as of April 30, 2026** in Workday’s Form 10-Q.

**Definition:** Workday says gross revenue retention measures “the percentage of recurring revenue retained from existing customers.” It is calculated by comparing ARR from the same customer set at the current period-end to total ARR for those customers at the corresponding prior period-end. It includes losses from product or customer churn, but **excludes add-ons and net expansions**, including volume and price adjustments.

Workday’s ARR basis: annualized value of active subscription contracts, annualized as total contract value divided by contract days and multiplied by 365; excludes certain short-term/non-core contracts; calculated on a constant-currency basis.

Sources: Workday Form 10-Q for quarter ended April 30, 2026, SEC filing; Workday SEC comment-response letter defining gross and net revenue retention.
