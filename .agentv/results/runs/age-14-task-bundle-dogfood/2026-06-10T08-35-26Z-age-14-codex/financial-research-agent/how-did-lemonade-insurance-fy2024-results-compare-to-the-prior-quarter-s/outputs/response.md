Lemonade’s FY2024 results were slightly better than the full-year guidance it gave with Q3 2024.

| Metric | Q3 FY2024 guidance | FY2024 actual | Compare |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| In-force premium | $940-$944m | $943.7m | Within range, near high end |
| Gross earned premium | $823-$826m | $827.3m | Beat high end by $1.3m |
| Revenue | $522-$524m | $526.5m | Beat high end by $2.5m |
| Adjusted EBITDA loss | $(155)-$(151)m | $(149.7)m | Better than high end by $1.3m less loss |
| Stock-based comp. | $64m | $64.5m | Slightly above |
| Capex | $10m | $9.4m | Slightly below |

Business context: Q4 was described as Lemonade’s “best quarter ever,” with $27m of adjusted free cash flow, IFP up 26% YoY to about $944m, revenue up 29%, Q4 gross loss ratio of 63%, and FY2024 adjusted free cash flow of $48m. Sources: Lemonade Q3 2024 shareholder letter guidance table and Q4/FY2024 shareholder letter results table.
