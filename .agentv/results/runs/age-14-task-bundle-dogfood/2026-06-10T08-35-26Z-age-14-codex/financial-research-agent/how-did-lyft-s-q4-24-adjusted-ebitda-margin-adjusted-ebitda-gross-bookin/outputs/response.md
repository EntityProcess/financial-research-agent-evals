Lyft **beat** Q4’24 Adjusted EBITDA margin guidance by about **26 bps**.

Calculation:
- Actual Q4’24: Adjusted EBITDA **$112.8M** / Gross Bookings **$4,278.9M** = **2.64%**. Lyft reported this rounded as **2.6%**.
- Q3’24 guidance midpoint: Adjusted EBITDA midpoint **$102.5M** / Gross Bookings midpoint **$4,315M** = **2.38%**.
- Difference: **2.64% - 2.38% = 0.26 percentage points**, or **~26 bps beat**.
