Airbnb annual take rate = Revenue / Gross Booking Value:

FY 2022: **13.3%** = $8.4B revenue / $63.2B GBV. Airbnb reported FY 2022 revenue of $8.4B and GBV of $63.2B in its Q4 2022 shareholder letter filed with the SEC. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000119312523039008/d451233dex991.htm)

FY 2023: **13.5%** = $9.917B revenue / $73.252B GBV. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972025000010/abnb-20241231.htm)

FY 2024: **13.6%** = $11.102B revenue / $81.784B GBV. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972025000010/abnb-20241231.htm)

Trend: Airbnb’s take rate was broadly stable but edged up modestly from **13.3% in 2022 to 13.6% in 2024**.
