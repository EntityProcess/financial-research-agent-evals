Netflix’s global Average Revenue per Paying User/member (reported as average monthly revenue per paying membership, ARM) rose modestly from **$10.82 in 2019** to **$11.70 in 2024**.

| Year | Avg. monthly revenue per paying membership |
|---|---:|
| 2019 | **$10.82** |
| 2020 | **$10.91** |
| 2021 | **$11.67** |
| 2022 | **$11.76** |
| 2023 | **$11.64** |
| 2024 | **$11.70** |

**Change 2019 to 2024:** **+$0.88/month**, or about **+8.1%**. Annualized, that is roughly **$129.84 per paying membership in 2019** versus **$140.40 in 2024**.

Key drivers cited by Netflix: 2021 ARM increased due to **price changes and favorable FX**; 2024 streaming revenue growth was helped by **higher average paying memberships and price increases**, partly offset by **unfavorable FX**. Netflix also notes that non-subscription streaming revenues, such as ads and other sources, were **not material** in 2022-2024. Sources: Netflix 2021 Form 10-K and 2024 Form 10-K via SEC.
