KKR’s March 2025 Series D mandatory convertible preferred stock terms were:

| Term | Summary |
|---|---|
| Security | 6.25% Series D Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock, planned NYSE symbol `KKR PR D` |
| Offering size | Priced at **$2.25 billion**, or **45,000,000 shares**, upsized from $1.50 billion; underwriters had a **6,750,000-share / $337.5 million** over-allotment option. KKR later issued **51,750,000 shares**, meaning the option was exercised in full, for **$2.5875 billion aggregate liquidation preference**. |
| Closing / issuance date | Expected and actual issuance date: **March 7, 2025**. |
| Price / liquidation preference | **$50.00 per share** public offering price and **$50.00 per share liquidation preference**. |
| Dividend rights | **Cumulative dividends at 6.25% annually** on the $50 liquidation preference, payable when, as and if declared. Dividend dates: March 1, June 1, September 1 and December 1, beginning June 1, 2025. Full quarterly dividend equals **$0.78125/share**; initial stub dividend was **$0.7292/share**. KKR may pay declared dividends in cash or common stock. |
| Conversion terms | Unless earlier converted, each preferred share automatically converts around **March 1, 2028** into **0.3312 to 0.4140 KKR common shares**, based on the 20-trading-day VWAP period before conversion, subject to anti-dilution adjustments. Holders may convert earlier at the minimum conversion rate, with special treatment upon a fundamental change. |
| Voting rights | Generally **no voting rights**, except as required by law and for protective provisions. If dividends are unpaid for six or more dividend periods, holders vote with other voting preferred stock to elect **two additional directors**. KKR also needs a **two-thirds preferred-holder vote** for specified adverse amendments, senior stock authorization, or certain mergers/reclassifications. |
| Purpose / use of proceeds | Net proceeds were estimated at **about $2.20 billion**, or **$2.53 billion** with full option exercise, to acquire additional equity interests in core private-equity portfolio companies reported in KKR’s Strategic Holdings segment and for general corporate purposes. |

Sources: KKR pricing release via Nasdaq for size, price, proceeds, purpose, dividend and conversion terms; SEC prospectus supplement for detailed dividend, liquidation, conversion and voting terms; KKR Form 8-K for actual March 7, 2025 issuance of 51.75 million shares and $2.5875 billion aggregate liquidation preference.
