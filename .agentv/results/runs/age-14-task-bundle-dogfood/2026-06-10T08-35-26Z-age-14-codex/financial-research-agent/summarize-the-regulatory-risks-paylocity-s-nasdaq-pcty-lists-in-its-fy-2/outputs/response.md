Paylocity’s FY 2024 10-K lists these main regulatory risks:

- **Changing HCM/payroll regulations:** tax, benefits, wage-and-hour, employment, and other U.S./international rules may require product changes, raise compliance costs, slow or block new product launches, or reduce the value/need for existing products. Paylocity says these changes could adversely affect business, operating results, or financial condition. ([investors.paylocity.com](https://investors.paylocity.com/node/14801/html))

- **Money-transmitter / money-services regulation:** new statutes or changed interpretations could require registration or licensing, restrict business activities, affect how Paylocity handles or invests client funds, and expose it to civil/criminal fines, penalties, legal costs, and reputational damage if regulators deem it unlicensed. ([investors.paylocity.com](https://investors.paylocity.com/node/14801/html))

- **AI and machine-learning regulation:** products using AI/ML may face current or future regulatory requirements, ethical/social scrutiny, misuse risk, and potential litigation involving IP, privacy, employment, civil-rights, or other claims. Noncompliance or failure to meet client expectations could lead to investigations, fines, penalties, damages, negative publicity, and reputational harm. ([investors.paylocity.com](https://investors.paylocity.com/node/14801/html))

- **Data privacy and security laws:** Paylocity processes employee/client personal data and is subject to federal, state, and international privacy rules, including HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws. The filing notes penalties can be significant, citing GDPR fines of up to **4% of worldwide revenue** for the prior year. ([investors.paylocity.com](https://investors.paylocity.com/node/14801/html))

- **Privacy-law interpretation/enforcement risk:** evolving or adverse interpretations could impose new obligations or prevent services in some jurisdictions; rising enforcement actions could bring penalties, legal liability, governmental oversight, increased costs, business restrictions, and reputation damage. ([investors.paylocity.com](https://investors.paylocity.com/node/14801/html))

- **Tax-law risk:** new or retroactive federal, state, or local tax rules for electronically delivered services, especially sales/use taxes, could increase service costs. If tax authorities determine taxes were unpaid, Paylocity could owe back taxes, interest, penalties, and future taxes. ([investors.paylocity.com](https://investors.paylocity.com/node/14801/html))
