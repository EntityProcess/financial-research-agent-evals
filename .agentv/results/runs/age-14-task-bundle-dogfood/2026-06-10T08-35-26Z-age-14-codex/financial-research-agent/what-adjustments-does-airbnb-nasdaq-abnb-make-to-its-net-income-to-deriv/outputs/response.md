Airbnb defines **Adjusted EBITDA** as **net income adjusted for**:

- Provision for income taxes
- Other expense, net
- Interest income
- Depreciation and amortization
- Stock-based compensation expense
- Acquisition-related impacts from changes in fair value of contingent consideration
- Lodging taxes, host withholding taxes, and transactional taxes, net
- Stock-settlement obligations related to the IPO

In its Q2 2025 10-Q reconciliation, Airbnb started with **net income of $642 million** and adjusted by these items to reach **Adjusted EBITDA of $1.043 billion** for the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Source: Airbnb Q2 2025 Form 10-Q / shareholder materials, non-GAAP definition and reconciliation.
