As of **Dec. 31, 2024**, Shift4 disclosed a clear vendor concentration risk:

- **North America merchant processing is facilitated by one vendor**.
- In the **U.S., EU, and Canada**, Shift4 depends on a **single third-party processor** for merchant authorization, processing, risk and chargeback monitoring, accounting, clearing, and settlement.
- A **majority of revenue comes from card transaction processing**, so disruption or loss of that vendor could materially affect operations.
- Shift4 also relies on a **member-bank sponsor** in North America; if the sponsor agreement is breached and terminated, Shift4 would have **180 days** to find an alternative sponsor.

Source: Shift4 Payments 2024 Form 10-K, “Concentration Risk” and risk factor disclosures.
