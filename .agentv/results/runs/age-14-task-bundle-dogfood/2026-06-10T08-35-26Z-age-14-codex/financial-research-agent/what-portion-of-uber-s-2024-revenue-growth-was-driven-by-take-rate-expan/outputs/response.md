Using revenue / Gross Bookings as Uber’s effective take rate:

- 2023: revenue `$37.281B`, Gross Bookings `$137.865B` → take rate `27.04%`
- 2024: revenue `$43.978B`, Gross Bookings `$162.773B` → take rate `27.02%`
- Revenue growth: `$6.697B`
- Gross Bookings growth: `$24.908B`

At the 2023 take rate, the 2024 bookings increase would have produced about `$6.736B` of added revenue. Actual revenue growth was `$6.697B`, so take-rate/mix was about `-$39M`.

**Answer:** roughly **101% of Uber’s 2024 revenue growth came from volume/Gross Bookings growth**, while **take-rate expansion contributed about -1%**. In plain terms, there was **no meaningful take-rate expansion**; effective take rate was slightly lower.

Uber’s own filing says 2024 revenue rose 18%, “primarily attributable” to 18% Gross Bookings growth, which was driven by Mobility and Delivery trip volumes, with some contra-revenue business model changes offsetting growth.
