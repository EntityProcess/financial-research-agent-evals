Using Boeing’s 2024 Form 10-K:

- 2024 total debt: **$53.864 billion** [SEC 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000001292725000015/ba-20241231.htm)
- 2024 net loss: **$11.829 billion** [SEC 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000001292725000015/ba-20241231.htm)
- 2024 interest and debt expense: **$2.725 billion** [SEC 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000001292725000015/ba-20241231.htm)

Calculation, assuming no incremental tax benefit:

`$53.864B debt * 3.0% = $1.616B additional annual interest expense`

Impact:

- Net income would fall by about **$1.616 billion**
- Boeing’s 2024 net loss would widen from **$11.829 billion** to about **$13.445 billion**
- Percent impact: **13.7%** of 2024 net loss, calculated as `$1.616B / $11.829B`

So, refinancing all 2024 debt at rates 3 percentage points higher would reduce Boeing’s net income by approximately **$1.6 billion**, worsening the 2024 loss by about **13.7%**.
